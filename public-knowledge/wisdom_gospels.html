Wisdom Gospels: The Inner Teachings of Yeshua

This page presents three foundational Wisdom Gospels—The Gospel of Philip, The Gospel of Mary, and The Gospel of Thomas—followed by seven companion texts from the same early stream: the Gospel of the Hebrews, Thunder, Perfect Mind, the Hymn of the Pearl, the Odes of Solomon, the Gospel of Truth, the Dialogue of the Savior, and the Gospel of the Egyptians.
For each key concept, you will find:
1. The words of the text (rendered from the original sources)
2. Plain English Meaning (practical philosophy for daily life)
Each companion text also notes where it echoes the sayings of The Living Way.

1. The Gospel of Philip

A collection of teachings from the Nag Hammadi library, probably third century. Most of it is the author's own teaching; Jesus speaks only now and then.

Spiritual Maturity vs. Mere Conversion

From the Gospel of Philip: "A slave seeks only to be free; he does not hope to gain his master's estate. But a son is not only a son: he lays claim to his father's inheritance. Those who inherit the dead are dead themselves, and what they inherit is dead. Those who inherit the living are alive."
Plain English Meaning: Joining a religion doesn't change who you are; it just changes what you call yourself. Don't act like a servant begging a distant God for freedom. Realize that you share in the divine life ("the son"), which means you already have a claim on it.

Sowing in Winter (The World) to Reap in Summer (Eternity)

From the Gospel of Philip: "Those who sow in winter reap in summer. The winter is the world; the summer is the other realm. Let us sow in the world, so that we may reap in the summer. Whoever tries to reap in winter will not reap, but only pull up young shoots."
Plain English Meaning: Life on earth is the season for planting, not for harvest. If you demand perfect comfort from the material world, you will be disappointed. Do the inner work now; its fruit comes in its own season.

The Illusion of Dualities and Names

From the Gospel of Philip: "Light and darkness, life and death, right and left are brothers of one another; they cannot be separated. So the good are not good, nor the evil evil, nor is life life, nor death death. The names given to worldly things are very deceptive: they turn the heart from what is real to what is not. Whoever hears the word 'God' does not perceive what is real, but what is not."
Plain English Meaning: Language traps us. We treat opposites like good and evil as separate armies, but they belong to one fabric. Even holy words like "God" are only pointers, not the thing itself. To understand, you have to look past the word to what it points at.

The Rulers and the Trap of Names

From the Gospel of Philip: "The rulers wanted to deceive humanity, because they saw that it was kin to what is truly good. They took the names of the good and gave them to what is not good, to deceive people by names and bind them to what is not good. They wanted to take the free and make them their slaves forever."
Plain English Meaning: The powers that run the world (status, money, approval) borrow the language of the good to sell you something that is not. Seeing through the borrowed names is the first step to freedom.

Resurrection Must Happen Now

From the Gospel of Philip: "Those who say they will first die and then rise are mistaken. If they do not receive the resurrection while they are alive, they will receive nothing when they die. While we are in this world, we must gain the resurrection for ourselves, so that when we take off the flesh we will be found in rest."
Plain English Meaning: Don't wait until you die to be "saved." Resurrection is waking up now. If you live your whole life asleep, dying won't fix it. Awakening happens while you are still in a body.

The Mystery of the Bridal Chamber (Union)

From the Gospel of Philip: "If the woman had not separated from the man, she would not die with the man. Their separation was the beginning of death. Christ came to repair the separation that was there from the beginning and to join the two. Those who have been united in the bridal chamber will no longer be separated. The holy of holies is the bridal chamber."
Plain English Meaning: The ultimate goal is union. This isn't about marriage; it's about healing the split within: masculine and feminine, spirit and soul, inner and outer. When you stop fighting yourself, you enter the "bridal chamber" of peace.

Mary Magdalene and the Kiss

From the Gospel of Philip, with the words of the Savior: "The companion of the Savior is Mary Magdalene. He loved her more than all the disciples. The disciples said to him, 'Why do you love her more than all of us?' The Savior answered, 'Why do I not love you as I love her? When a blind person and one who sees are both in the dark, they are no different from each other. When the light comes, the one who sees will see the light, and the blind one will stay in the dark.' … It is by a kiss that the perfect conceive and give birth."
Plain English Meaning: The kiss in Philip symbolizes the passing of spiritual knowledge from one to another. The disciples' question is about rank; the Savior's answer is about sight. What matters is not position but the capacity to see the light when it comes. (The manuscript is damaged in this passage; some words are reconstructed.)

God the Dyer and God the Devourer

From the Gospel of Philip: "God is a dyer. As good dyes, called 'true', dissolve into the things dyed in them, so it is with those whom God has dyed: they become imperishable. … God devours people. That is why people are sacrificed to him. Before people were sacrificed, animals were sacrificed, because those they were offered to were not gods."
Plain English Meaning: These are startling images of transformation. The divine does not decorate the surface; it soaks all the way through, until what it touches takes on its color. To be "devoured" is the dissolving of the small, separate self into something larger.

Perception Creates Reality

From the Gospel of Philip: "No one can see anything of what truly is without becoming like it. You saw the Spirit, and you became spirit. You saw Christ, and you became Christ. You saw the Father, and you will become Father. Here you see everything and do not see yourself; but there you see yourself, and what you see you will become."
Plain English Meaning: In the physical world, looking at a tree doesn't make you a tree. In the spiritual life, you become what you behold. You can't study the divine from outside; you have to take on its likeness.

The Root of Evil is Ignorance

From the Gospel of Philip: "Ignorance is the mother of every evil. As long as the root of wickedness is hidden, it is strong; when it is recognized, it dissolves; when it is revealed, it perishes. Let each of us dig down to the root of evil within and pull it out of the heart. As the Word said: 'If you know the truth, the truth will make you free.'"
Plain English Meaning: Evil isn't mainly a force attacking you from outside; it grows from not knowing yourself. Your "demons" (fear, hate, compulsion) are strong only while they stay hidden. Once you see them clearly, they lose their hold.

2. The Gospel of Mary (Magdalene)

A second-century gospel known from a damaged Coptic codex that reached Berlin in 1896, and from two Greek fragments. Several of its pages are lost.

The Nature of Sin and Matter

The Words of the Savior: "There is no sin as such. It is you who make sin when you act in ways that confuse your nature, which is called sin. That is why the Good came among you, to restore every nature to its root. Matter gave birth to a passion that has no likeness, because it came from what is contrary to nature; then a disturbance arises in the whole body. Peace be with you. Receive my peace within you."
Plain English Meaning: Sin isn't about breaking religious rules. It is the confusion and pain that come from acting against your true nature and clinging to what passes. The cure is not punishment but restoration: returning each thing to its root.

The Kingdom is Inside (The Son of Man)

The Words of the Savior: "Beware that no one leads you astray, saying, 'Look here!' or 'Look there!' For the Son of Man is within you. Follow him. Those who seek him will find him. Go, then, and preach the good news of the Kingdom."
Plain English Meaning: Stop looking for a savior to arrive from the sky. The "Son of Man", the true human, is already within you. You don't need to go anywhere; you need to look within and follow what you find.

No Law Beyond the Teaching

The Words of the Savior: "Do not lay down any rule beyond what I have appointed for you, and do not give a law like the lawgiver, lest you be bound by it."
Plain English Meaning: Rules multiply when the spirit is forgotten. The Savior warns against turning his teaching into a new legal code. The more laws you make, the more you become their prisoner.

Emotional Stability in Crisis

The Words of Mary: "Do not weep and do not grieve or be in two minds, for his grace will be with you completely and will protect you. Rather, let us praise his greatness, for he has prepared us and made us truly human."
Plain English Meaning: When the Savior left, the disciples were afraid. Mary steadied them. True spiritual maturity shows up as steadiness when things go wrong: remembering the inner resources ("grace") you already have.

Vision Occurs in the Mind (Nous)

The Words of the Savior to Mary: "Blessed are you, for you did not waver at seeing me. For where the mind is, there is the treasure. … One does not see through the soul, nor through the spirit, but through the mind that is between the two. That is what sees the vision."
Plain English Meaning: Spiritual vision is neither emotional excitement nor mere thinking. It happens in the "nous", the clear seeing mind between feeling and spirit. Where your attention rests, there your treasure is.

The Powers Question the Soul

Mary's Vision: The Soul's Ascent: "Desire said, 'I did not see you coming down, but now I see you going up. Why do you lie, since you belong to me?' The soul answered, 'I saw you. You did not see me or recognize me. I served you as a garment, and you did not know me.' … Ignorance said, 'You are bound. Do not judge!' The soul answered, 'Why do you judge me, when I have not judged? I was bound, but I have not bound.'"
Plain English Meaning: As you grow, you meet your own patterns (craving, ignorance, anger), which the text calls "powers." They claim you as their own. You don't defeat them by fighting; you see them clearly and recognize that they are something you wore, not who you are.

The Soul's Declaration of Freedom

Mary's Vision: The Soul's Ascent: "What binds me has been slain, and what surrounds me has been overcome. My desire has come to an end, and ignorance has died. I was set free from a world, and from the chain of forgetting, which is passing. From now on I will reach the rest of the time, of the season, of the age, in silence."
Plain English Meaning: This is the moment you realize you are not your compulsions or your personality. What held you is seen through, and what remains is rest, in silence.

The Conflict of Gender and Jealousy

The Words of Levi: "Peter, you have always been hot-tempered. Now I see you arguing against the woman as if she were an enemy. But if the Savior made her worthy, who are you to reject her? Surely the Savior knows her very well. That is why he loved her more than us. Rather, let us be ashamed, put on the perfect human, and go out and preach the good news, laying down no other rule or law beyond what the Savior said."
Plain English Meaning: Peter is angry that the Savior taught Mary what he had not taught the men. The scene sets institution (Peter) against inner vision (Mary). Levi's answer is simple: capacity, not rank, is what counts, and no new laws should be added to the teaching.

3. The Gospel of Thomas

A collection of 114 sayings from the Nag Hammadi library, with earlier Greek fragments from Oxyrhynchus. Saying numbers follow the standard division.

The Burden of Interpretation

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 1): "Whoever finds the meaning of these sayings will not taste death."
Plain English Meaning: You can't just passively believe these teachings; you have to work them out for yourself. It's a riddle. If you do the work of finding their meaning, you discover a life death cannot reach.

The Emotional Arc of Awakening

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 2): "Let the one who seeks not stop seeking until they find. When they find, they will be troubled. When they are troubled, they will marvel, and they will reign over the All."
Plain English Meaning: Finding the truth is unsettling at first. It breaks up comfortable stories, and you will feel "troubled." If you stay with it, the trouble turns into wonder, and wonder into freedom.

The Kingdom is a State of Consciousness

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 3): "If those who lead you say, 'Look, the Kingdom is in the sky,' then the birds will get there before you. If they say, 'It is in the sea,' then the fish will get there before you. Rather, the Kingdom is inside you, and it is outside you. When you know yourselves, you will be known."
Plain English Meaning: God isn't in a distant place. The Kingdom is a way of seeing that is present right here, inside you and around you. You just need to notice it.

Authenticity is Survival

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 70): "If you bring forth what is within you, what you bring forth will save you. If you do not bring forth what is within you, what you do not bring forth will destroy you."
Plain English Meaning: If you have a gift, a truth, or a purpose inside you, you must express it. Repressing your true self damages you; bringing it forth heals you.

Non-Duality (Making the Two One)

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 22): "When you make the two one, and make the inside like the outside and the outside like the inside, and the above like the below … then you will enter the Kingdom."
Plain English Meaning: Stop living a double life. Stop separating "spiritual" life from "real" life. When you are the same person inside and outside, you are whole.

Spiritual Sleepwalking (The Drunk World)

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 28): "I stood in the middle of the world and appeared to them in the flesh. I found them all drunk; I found none of them thirsty. My soul ached for the children of humanity, because they are blind in their hearts and do not see."
Plain English Meaning: Most people are "drunk" on distraction: money, status, entertainment. They are empty but don't feel thirsty, because the distraction never stops. Waking up begins with noticing your own thirst.

Non-Attachment (The Passerby)

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 42): "Be passersby."
Plain English Meaning: Live in the world, but don't settle into it as if it were permanent. Treat life as a journey, not a fortress.

The Futility of Ritual without Honesty

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 6): "Do not lie, and do not do what you hate, for everything is plain in the sight of heaven. Nothing hidden will fail to be revealed, and nothing covered will stay uncovered."
Plain English Meaning: Asked about fasting and prayer, Jesus answers with honesty instead. Rituals mean little if you are living a lie. Don't lie, and don't do what you hate.

Taming the Lion

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 7): "Blessed is the lion that the human eats, so that the lion becomes human. And cursed is the human that the lion eats; the lion will become human."
Plain English Meaning: You have animal drives: hunger, anger, desire. If they consume you, you lose your humanity. If you take them in and transform them, their strength becomes yours.

Presence Over Prophecy

The Words of Yeshua (Thomas 113): "It will not come by watching for it. No one will say, 'Look, here it is,' or 'Look, there it is.' Rather, the Kingdom of the Father is spread out upon the earth, and people do not see it."
Plain English Meaning: Stop waiting for the end of the world. The Kingdom is already here, spread out over the earth. We miss it because we are always looking for something else.

4. The Gospel of the Hebrews

An early Jewish-Christian gospel, lost except for quotations in Clement of Alexandria, Origen, and Jerome (second to fifth centuries).

Seeking Ends in Rest

The Words of Yeshua: "Whoever seeks will not stop until they find. Having found, they will marvel. Having marveled, they will reign. And having reigned, they will rest."
Plain English Meaning: This is the same map as Thomas 2, with its last step restored. The point of the Way is not power over anything. Seeking ends in rest: a settledness that no longer needs to grasp.

My Mother, the Holy Spirit

The Words of Yeshua: "Just now my mother, the Holy Spirit, took me by one of my hairs and carried me away to the great mountain Tabor."
Plain English Meaning: The earliest Jewish followers of Jesus could speak of the Spirit as Mother. In Hebrew the word for Spirit, ruach, is usually feminine. The divine is not only the Father who sends; it is also the Mother who carries.

You Are My Rest

The Words of the Spirit at the Baptism: "My son, in all the prophets I was waiting for you, that you would come and I might rest in you. For you are my rest. You are my firstborn son."
Plain English Meaning: At the baptism, the Spirit does not command; she rests. The Way works in both directions: you come to rest in the divine, and the divine comes to rest in you.

Joy Only in Love

The Words of Yeshua: "Never be joyful except when you look upon your brother in love."
Plain English Meaning: Joy that depends on someone else losing is not joy. Real gladness comes from seeing another person clearly and wishing them well.

In The Living Way: 52, Return to the Mother, 40, The Return.

5. Thunder, Perfect Mind

A poem from the Nag Hammadi library, spoken by a divine feminine voice in paradoxes.

The Voice Beyond Opposites

The Voice of Thunder: "I am the first and the last. I am the honored one and the scorned one. I am the prostitute and the holy one. I am the wife and the virgin. I am the mother and the daughter."
Plain English Meaning: The divine cannot be kept on one side of our categories. She is found in what we honor and in what we despise. Whoever tries to hold only half of her loses her altogether.

The Silence and the Voice

The Voice of Thunder: "I am the silence that cannot be grasped, and the thought that is remembered often. I am the voice of many sounds, and the word of many forms. I am the speaking of my own name."
Plain English Meaning: This is the Mother as Silence: the quiet from which every word comes. You do not reach her by saying more. You reach her by listening beneath what is said.

Do Not Be Ignorant of Me

The Voice of Thunder: "Look upon me, you who reflect upon me. Be on your guard. Do not be ignorant of me. I am knowledge and ignorance."
Plain English Meaning: Wisdom is not far away. She is present in your attention, waiting to be noticed. Even your ignorance belongs to her: it is the place where knowing begins.

In The Living Way: 25, The Womb of Silence, 42, The Birth of the Two, 52, Return to the Mother.

6. The Hymn of the Pearl

A parable preserved in the Acts of Thomas (third century), sung in the first person by a king's son.

Sent Down for the Pearl

From the Hymn: "My parents took off my robe of glory and sent me down to Egypt, to bring back the one pearl that lies in the sea, guarded by the serpent. When you return, they said, you will put on your robe again."
Plain English Meaning: Each soul comes into the world for something precious. The robe it leaves behind is not lost; it is waiting for its return.

Forgetting

From the Hymn: "I put on their clothes and ate their food. I forgot that I was a king's son, and I served their king. I forgot the pearl for which I had been sent, and I fell into a deep sleep."
Plain English Meaning: This is the human condition in one sentence: we take on the clothes of the world, and in time we forget who wears them. The sleep is not a punishment. It is what happens when we forget.

The Letter

From the Hymn: "Wake, and rise from your sleep. Remember that you are a king's son. See whom you have served in slavery. Remember the pearl for which you were sent."
Plain English Meaning: Something always comes to wake us: a word, a loss, a moment of beauty, a saying read at the right time. The message is simple: remember.

The Robe That Was My Image

From the Hymn: "When I saw the robe, it became like a mirror of myself. I saw all of myself in it, and in it I saw myself whole. We were two, and yet we were one in one likeness."
Plain English Meaning: Returning home is not becoming someone else. It is meeting the self you always were and recognizing it. The two become one.

In The Living Way: 14, The Return to the Unborn, 40, The Return, 2, The Two Become One.

7. The Odes of Solomon

Often called the earliest Christian hymnbook, written in Syriac or Greek around 100–125 CE.

The Milk of the Spirit

Ode 19: "A cup of milk was offered to me, and I drank it in the sweetness of the Lord's kindness. The Son is the cup, the Father is the one who was milked, and the Holy Spirit is she who milked him."
Plain English Meaning: The image is startling on purpose. The divine nourishes like a mother, and the Spirit is the one who gives that nourishment to the world. Faith here is not a set of claims; it is being fed.

Putting On the Lord

Ode 7: "He became like me, that I might receive him. In form he seemed like me, that I might put him on."
Plain English Meaning: The Living One does not stand far off to be admired. He becomes close enough to be worn, like a garment, until his life and yours are no longer separate.

The Living Spring

Ode 30: "Fill for yourselves water from the living spring of the Lord, for it has been opened to you. Come, all you who are thirsty, and drink, and rest beside the spring of the Lord."
Plain English Meaning: The spring is already open. Nothing needs to be earned. The only task is to come, drink, and rest.

The Stream That Became a River

Ode 6: "A stream went out and became a river, great and broad. It swept over everything, and it brought it to the Temple."
Plain English Meaning: Small beginnings in the inner life grow. What starts as a trickle of attention becomes a river that carries the whole of a life home.

In The Living Way: 34, The Great River, 52, Return to the Mother.

8. The Gospel of Truth

A meditation from the Nag Hammadi library, probably second century, on forgetting and remembering.

The Fog of Forgetting

From the Gospel of Truth: "Ignorance of the Father brought about anguish and terror, and the anguish grew thick like a fog, so that no one was able to see."
Plain English Meaning: Most of our suffering is not caused by evil forces. It comes from forgetting where we come from. Fear thickens that forgetting until we cannot see through it.

Waking from the Nightmare

From the Gospel of Truth: "It is as if people were asleep and found themselves in disturbing dreams: fleeing, or pursued, or falling from a height. Then they wake, and they see nothing, for the dreams were nothing."
Plain English Meaning: Many of our terrors belong to the dream. Waking does not defeat them; it shows they were never solid. This is what the Way means by remembering.

When the Father Is Known

From the Gospel of Truth: "Since the lack came about because the Father was not known, when the Father is known the lack will no longer exist. As with a person's ignorance: when they come to know, their ignorance vanishes of itself, as darkness vanishes when light appears."
Plain English Meaning: You do not have to fight the darkness. You only have to turn on the light. Knowing does the work that struggle cannot.

In The Living Way: 7, The Unforced Kingdom, 40, The Return.

9. The Dialogue of the Savior

A fragmentary dialogue from the Nag Hammadi library between the Savior and his disciples, among them Mary.

The Lamp of the Body

The Words of Yeshua: "The lamp of the body is the mind. As long as the things within you are set in order, your bodies are lights."
Plain English Meaning: When your inner life is in order, it shows. Clarity inside becomes light outside, in how you speak, act, and see.

Whoever Knows Themselves

The Words of Yeshua: "Each one of you who has known yourself has seen it."
Plain English Meaning: The 'place of life' the disciples ask about is not somewhere else. Knowing yourself is how you see it.

The Woman Who Understood

From the Dialogue: "She spoke this as a woman who had understood completely."
Plain English Meaning: The text says this of Mary. As in the Gospel of Mary, understanding, not rank, is what makes a disciple.

In The Living Way: 10, The Single Eye.

10. The Gospel of the Egyptians (as quoted by Clement)

A lost gospel known from brief quotations in Clement of Alexandria (around 200 CE). It is not the Nag Hammadi text of the same name.

When the Two Become One

The Words of Yeshua to Salome: "When you trample on the garment of shame, and when the two become one, and the male with the female is neither male nor female."
Plain English Meaning: The same teaching as Thomas 22 and 37. Wholeness comes when you stop dividing yourself, and when the roles you wear no longer define you.

In The Living Way: 2, The Two Become One.
