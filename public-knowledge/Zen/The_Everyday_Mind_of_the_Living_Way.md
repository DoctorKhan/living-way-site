# The Everyday Mind of the Living Way

### *Original teachings after the spirit of Zen — not classical translations*

---

These are **original** texts for daily practice. They borrow **nothing** verbatim from historical koan collections or copyrighted modern Zen books. They echo **one** intention: **direct seeing in ordinary work**, without rank, without attainment theater, without a master to kneel to.

**Formal moves borrowed from Zen (shape only):**

- **Ordinary mind** — the sacred is the task done fully, not a special state advertised.
- **Direct pointing** — show the cup, the bowl, the breath; do not deliver the conclusion.
- **Don't-know** — hold the question without rushing to doctrine (*this felt real; I may still be wrong*).
- **Continuous practice** — sweep, wash, repair, serve tea; the path is the next honest motion.
- **Nothing to attain** — you are not broken goods waiting for a badge; stop performing enlightenment.

**Anti-patterns (Zen history warns against these too):**

- Koans as puzzles with secret answers (the counterfeit hands you the invoice).
- Lineage worship, transmission certificates, and "my roshi said."
- Special states sold as rank (activation caste — the novel's registry in monastic dress).

---

## I · Ordinary mind

Before you sought, mountains were mountains.  
While you sought, mountains became gold, tests, and proof.  
After you stop performing the search,  
mountains are mountains again —  
but your hands know the difference,  
and you need not speak of it.

---

## II · The washed bowl

When the meal is finished,  
wash the bowl.  
Not to become holy.  
Not to be seen washing.  

The teaching is the water  
running off the rim.  
Leave no story about it.

---

## III · Direct pointing

A student asked what the Way is.  
The host set a cup on the table  
and said nothing.  

The student waited for the answer.  
The cup cooled.  
The answer was never in the cup —  
only in whether the student  
would stay with what was actually there.

---

## IV · Don't-know

Does the field have a soul?  
Does the file tell the truth?  
Does the vision prove God?  

Say: *I don't know yet.*  
That is not weakness.  
That is the only gate  
that stays open.

---

## V · The swept floor

Sweep the floor you are standing on.  
Do not sweep the floor  
you will post about.  

Dust returns tomorrow.  
So what?  
The floor needed sweeping today.

---

## VI · Beginner again

On the first day you can hold the cup  
without gripping the story,  
treat that day as the whole practice.  

On the thousandth day  
you reach for the cup the same way —  
as if you had never mastered anything.

---

## VII · Tea after

You need not win the argument with yourself  
before you set the kettle.  

Pour for whoever is there.  
Drink while it is hot.  
The rest can wait  
in the silence that follows.
