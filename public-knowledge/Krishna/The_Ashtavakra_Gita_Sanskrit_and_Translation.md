# The Ashtavakra Gita: Selected Verses, Sanskrit and Translation

**Source:** The *Aṣṭāvakra Gītā* (also called the *Aṣṭāvakra Saṃhitā*) is a dialogue in Sanskrit between King Janaka and the sage Aṣṭāvakra, in twenty chapters and 298 verses. Its author and date are unknown. The Sanskrit below follows the text at [Sanskrit Documents — Aṣṭāvakra Gītā](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_giitaa/ashtgita.html). This page gives 48 of the 298 verses; the translations are our own.

---

## How to Read It

The Ashtavakra Gita speaks from the standpoint of awareness itself, where nothing was ever bound. Read from the small self, its claims sound impossible; read from the Light, they are simply true. The practices of the Living Way do not create this freedom; they shift where you stand, until it can be seen.

That is also the key to its hardest lines, such as “This alone is your bondage: that you keep practicing meditation” (1.15). From the standpoint of the one who practices, practice is the way. From the standpoint of awareness, there is no one who needs to get anywhere. Both are true at their own level; the text simply refuses to speak from the first.

In *The Living Way*, see [36, “The Way, the Truth”](The_Living_Way.html#the-way-the-truth) and [18, “The Unbinding”](The_Living_Way.html#the-unbinding). For practices that lead toward this standpoint, see the [Guide to the Way of the Living Jesus](living_way_guide.html).

In the Guide’s terms, this is the first of two doors: the fire of the eye, the Father’s door. Its companion for the second door, the Mother’s door of the senses, is the *Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra*; see “Only the Seen” in the [Guide](living_way_guide.html#practice-3-only-the-seen).

## Chapter 1 — The Teaching

*Janaka asks how freedom comes. Aṣṭāvakra answers, and gives the whole teaching at once.*

### 1.1 — Janaka asks

**Sanskrit:** कथं ज्ञानमवाप्नोति कथं मुक्तिर्भविष्यति ।  
वैराग्यं च कथं प्राप्तमेतद् ब्रूहि मम प्रभो ॥ १-१ ॥

**Transliteration:** *kathaṃ jñānamavāpnoti kathaṃ muktirbhaviṣyati* · *vairāgyaṃ ca kathaṃ prāptametad brūhi mama prabho*

**Translation:** How does one come to knowledge? How will freedom come? And how is dispassion reached? Tell me this, my lord.

---

### 1.2 — The first medicine

**Sanskrit:** मुक्तिमिच्छसि चेत्तात विषयान् विषवत्त्यज ।  
क्षमार्जवदयातोषसत्यं पीयूषवद् भज ॥ १-२ ॥

**Transliteration:** *muktimicchasi cettāta viṣayān viṣavattyaja* · *kṣamārjavadayātoṣasatyaṃ pīyūṣavad bhaja*

**Translation:** If you seek freedom, my child, shun the objects of the senses as poison. Drink forgiveness, honesty, kindness, contentment, and truth as nectar.

---

### 1.3 — Not the elements

**Sanskrit:** न पृथ्वी न जलं नाग्निर्न वायुर्द्यौर्न वा भवान् ।  
एषां साक्षिणमात्मानं चिद्रूपं विद्धि मुक्तये ॥ १-३ ॥

**Transliteration:** *na pṛthvī na jalaṃ nāgnirna vāyurdyaurna vā bhavān* · *eṣāṃ sākṣiṇamātmānaṃ cidrūpaṃ viddhi muktaye*

**Translation:** You are not earth, nor water, nor fire, nor air, nor space. To be free, know yourself as the witness of all these: awareness itself.

---

### 1.4 — Rest in awareness

**Sanskrit:** यदि देहं पृथक् कृत्य चिति विश्राम्य तिष्ठसि ।  
अधुनैव सुखी शान्तो बन्धमुक्तो भविष्यसि ॥ १-४ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yadi dehaṃ pṛthak kṛtya citi viśrāmya tiṣṭhasi* · *adhunaiva sukhī śānto bandhamukto bhaviṣyasi*

**Translation:** If you set the body apart and rest in awareness, then this very moment you will be happy, at peace, and free from bondage.

---

### 1.5 — No caste, no stage, no form

**Sanskrit:** न त्वं विप्रादिको वर्णो नाश्रमी नाक्षगोचरः ।  
असङ्गोऽसि निराकारो विश्वसाक्षी सुखी भव ॥ १-५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *na tvaṃ viprādiko varṇo nāśramī nākṣagocaraḥ* · *asaṅgo'si nirākāro viśvasākṣī sukhī bhava*

**Translation:** You belong to no caste and to no stage of life; you are not what the eyes can see. Unattached and formless, the witness of all, be happy.

---

### 1.6 — Not the doer

**Sanskrit:** धर्माधर्मौ सुखं दुःखं मानसानि न ते विभो ।  
न कर्तासि न भोक्तासि मुक्त एवासि सर्वदा ॥ १-६ ॥

**Transliteration:** *dharmādharmau sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ mānasāni na te vibho* · *na kartāsi na bhoktāsi mukta evāsi sarvadā*

**Translation:** Right and wrong, pleasure and pain belong to the mind, not to you, all-pervading one. You are not the doer, nor the one who reaps the fruit. You are always free.

---

### 1.7 — The only bondage

**Sanskrit:** एको द्रष्टासि सर्वस्य मुक्तप्रायोऽसि सर्वदा ।  
अयमेव हि ते बन्धो द्रष्टारं पश्यसीतरम् ॥ १-७ ॥

**Transliteration:** *eko draṣṭāsi sarvasya muktaprāyo'si sarvadā* · *ayameva hi te bandho draṣṭāraṃ paśyasītaram*

**Translation:** You are the one seer of all, and you are always already free. This alone is your bondage: that you see the seer as someone else.

---

### 1.8 — The serpent and the nectar

**Sanskrit:** अहं कर्तेत्यहंमानमहाकृष्णाहिदंशितः ।  
नाहं कर्तेति विश्वासामृतं पीत्वा सुखं चर ॥ १-८ ॥

**Transliteration:** *ahaṃ kartetyahaṃmānamahākṛṣṇāhidaṃśitaḥ* · *nāhaṃ karteti viśvāsāmṛtaṃ pītvā sukhaṃ cara*

**Translation:** You have been bitten by the great black serpent of the thought “I am the doer.” Drink the nectar of the trust “I am not the doer,” and be happy.

---

### 1.9 — The fire of certainty

**Sanskrit:** एको विशुद्धबोधोऽहमिति निश्चयवह्निना ।  
प्रज्वाल्याज्ञानगहनं वीतशोकः सुखी भव ॥ १-९ ॥

**Transliteration:** *eko viśuddhabodho'hamiti niścayavahninā* · *prajvālyājñānagahanaṃ vītaśokaḥ sukhī bhava*

**Translation:** “I am the one pure awareness”: with the fire of this certainty, burn down the forest of ignorance. Free from sorrow, be happy.

---

### 1.10 — The rope and the snake

**Sanskrit:** यत्र विश्वमिदं भाति कल्पितं रज्जुसर्पवत् ।  
आनन्दपरमानन्दः स बोधस्त्वं सुखं भव ॥ १-१० ॥

**Transliteration:** *yatra viśvamidaṃ bhāti kalpitaṃ rajjusarpavat* · *ānandaparamānandaḥ sa bodhastvaṃ sukhaṃ bhava*

**Translation:** That in which this world appears, imagined like a snake in a rope, that joy, that highest joy, that awareness, you are. Be happy.

---

### 1.11 — As you think, so you become

**Sanskrit:** मुक्ताभिमानी मुक्तो हि बद्धो बद्धाभिमान्यपि ।  
किंवदन्तीह सत्येयं या मतिः सा गतिर्भवेत् ॥ १-११ ॥

**Transliteration:** *muktābhimānī mukto hi baddho baddhābhimānyapi* · *kiṃvadantīha satyeyaṃ yā matiḥ sā gatirbhavet*

**Translation:** One who takes themselves to be free is free; one who takes themselves to be bound is bound. The saying is true: as you think, so you become.

---

### 1.12 — What the Self is

**Sanskrit:** आत्मा साक्षी विभुः पूर्ण एको मुक्तश्चिदक्रियः ।  
असङ्गो निःस्पृहः शान्तो भ्रमात्संसारवानिव ॥ १-१२ ॥

**Transliteration:** *ātmā sākṣī vibhuḥ pūrṇa eko muktaścidakriyaḥ* · *asaṅgo niḥspṛhaḥ śānto bhramātsaṃsāravāniva*

**Translation:** The Self is the witness: all-pervading, complete, one, free, awareness, actionless, unattached, desireless, at peace. Only through illusion does it seem caught in the world.

---

### 1.13 — Not the reflection

**Sanskrit:** कूटस्थं बोधमद्वैतमात्मानं परिभावय ।  
आभासोऽहं भ्रमं मुक्त्वा भावं बाह्यमथान्तरम् ॥ १-१३ ॥

**Transliteration:** *kūṭasthaṃ bodhamadvaitamātmānaṃ paribhāvaya* · *ābhāso'haṃ bhramaṃ muktvā bhāvaṃ bāhyamathāntaram*

**Translation:** Contemplate the Self as unmoving awareness, without a second. Let go of the illusion “I am the reflection,” and of every outer and inner state.

---

### 1.14 — The sword of knowing

**Sanskrit:** देहाभिमानपाशेन चिरं बद्धोऽसि पुत्रक ।  
बोधोऽहं ज्ञानखड्गेन तन्निकृत्य सुखी भव ॥ १-१४ ॥

**Transliteration:** *dehābhimānapāśena ciraṃ baddho'si putraka* · *bodho'haṃ jñānakhaḍgena tannikṛtya sukhī bhava*

**Translation:** For a long time, my child, you have been bound by the noose of taking yourself to be the body. Cut it with the sword of knowing “I am awareness,” and be happy.

---

### 1.15 — The bondage of practice

**Sanskrit:** निःसङ्गो निष्क्रियोऽसि त्वं स्वप्रकाशो निरञ्जनः ।  
अयमेव हि ते बन्धः समाधिमनुतिष्ठसि ॥ १-१५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *niḥsaṅgo niṣkriyo'si tvaṃ svaprakāśo nirañjanaḥ* · *ayameva hi te bandhaḥ samādhimanutiṣṭhasi*

**Translation:** You are unattached, actionless, self-luminous, spotless. This alone is your bondage: that you keep practicing meditation.

---

### 1.16 — Do not grow small

**Sanskrit:** त्वया व्याप्तमिदं विश्वं त्वयि प्रोतं यथार्थतः ।  
शुद्धबुद्धस्वरूपस्त्वं मा गमः क्षुद्रचित्तताम् ॥ १-१६ ॥

**Transliteration:** *tvayā vyāptamidaṃ viśvaṃ tvayi protaṃ yathārthataḥ* · *śuddhabuddhasvarūpastvaṃ mā gamaḥ kṣudracittatām*

**Translation:** This universe is pervaded by you and woven in you. Your nature is pure awareness. Do not fall into smallness of mind.

---

### 1.17 — Cool-hearted

**Sanskrit:** निरपेक्षो निर्विकारो निर्भरः शीतलाशयः ।  
अगाधबुद्धिरक्षुब्धो भव चिन्मात्रवासनः ॥ १-१७ ॥

**Transliteration:** *nirapekṣo nirvikāro nirbharaḥ śītalāśayaḥ* · *agādhabuddhirakṣubdho bhava cinmātravāsanaḥ*

**Translation:** Be free of need, free of change, at ease, cool-hearted, unshaken, of fathomless understanding, resting in awareness alone.

---

### 1.18 — Form and the formless

**Sanskrit:** साकारमनृतं विद्धि निराकारं तु निश्चलम् ।  
एतत्तत्त्वोपदेशेन न पुनर्भवसम्भवः ॥ १-१८ ॥

**Transliteration:** *sākāramanṛtaṃ viddhi nirākāraṃ tu niścalam* · *etattattvopadeśena na punarbhavasambhavaḥ*

**Translation:** Know that what has form is unreal, and what is formless is unmoving. Through this teaching of truth, there is no more coming back into becoming.

---

### 1.19 — The mirror

**Sanskrit:** यथैवादर्शमध्यस्थे रूपेऽन्तः परितस्तु सः ।  
तथैवाऽस्मिन् शरीरेऽन्तः परितः परमेश्वरः ॥ १-१९ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yathaivādarśamadhyasthe rūpe'ntaḥ paritastu saḥ* · *tathaivā'smin śarīre'ntaḥ paritaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

**Translation:** As a mirror is within and around the image reflected in it, so the Supreme Lord is within and around this body.

---

### 1.20 — Space in the jar

**Sanskrit:** एकं सर्वगतं व्योम बहिरन्तर्यथा घटे ।  
नित्यं निरन्तरं ब्रह्म सर्वभूतगणे तथा ॥ १-२० ॥

**Transliteration:** *ekaṃ sarvagataṃ vyoma bahirantaryathā ghaṭe* · *nityaṃ nirantaraṃ brahma sarvabhūtagaṇe tathā*

**Translation:** As the one all-pervading space is inside and outside a jar, so the eternal, unbroken Brahman is in all beings.

---

## Chapter 2 — Janaka Awakens

*Janaka speaks. Having heard the teaching, he recognizes what he is.*

### 2.1 — Wonderful!

**Sanskrit:** अहो निरञ्जनः शान्तो बोधोऽहं प्रकृतेः परः ।  
एतावन्तमहं कालं मोहेनैव विडम्बितः ॥ २-१ ॥

**Transliteration:** *aho nirañjanaḥ śānto bodho'haṃ prakṛteḥ paraḥ* · *etāvantamahaṃ kālaṃ mohenaiva viḍambitaḥ*

**Translation:** Wonderful! I am spotless, at peace, awareness itself, beyond nature. All this time I have been fooled by delusion.

---

### 2.2 — The one light

**Sanskrit:** यथा प्रकाशयाम्येको देहमेनं तथा जगत् ।  
अतो मम जगत्सर्वमथवा न च किञ्चन ॥ २-२ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yathā prakāśayāmyeko dehamenaṃ tathā jagat* · *ato mama jagatsarvamathavā na ca kiñcana*

**Translation:** As I alone give light to this body, so I give light to the world. So the whole world is mine, or nothing is.

---

### 2.4 — Waves and water

**Sanskrit:** यथा न तोयतो भिन्नास्तरङ्गाः फेनबुद्बुदाः ।  
आत्मनो न तथा भिन्नं विश्वमात्मविनिर्गतम् ॥ २-४ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yathā na toyato bhinnāstaraṅgāḥ phenabudbudāḥ* · *ātmano na tathā bhinnaṃ viśvamātmavinirgatam*

**Translation:** As waves, foam, and bubbles are not different from water, so the world that comes forth from the Self is not different from the Self.

---

### 2.7 — When the rope is known

**Sanskrit:** आत्माज्ञानाज्जगद्भाति आत्मज्ञानान्न भासते ।  
रज्ज्वज्ञानादहिर्भाति तज्ज्ञानाद् भासते न हि ॥ २-७ ॥

**Transliteration:** *ātmājñānājjagadbhāti ātmajñānānna bhāsate* · *rajjvajñānādahirbhāti tajjñānād bhāsate na hi*

**Translation:** Through not knowing the Self, the world appears; through knowing the Self, it does not. Through not knowing the rope, the snake appears; when the rope is known, the snake does not.

---

### 2.10 — Clay, water, gold

**Sanskrit:** मत्तो विनिर्गतं विश्वं मय्येव लयमेष्यति ।  
मृदि कुम्भो जले वीचिः  कनके कटकं यथा ॥ २-१० ॥

**Transliteration:** *matto vinirgataṃ viśvaṃ mayyeva layameṣyati* · *mṛdi kumbho jale vīciḥ  kanake kaṭakaṃ yathā*

**Translation:** The world came forth from me and will dissolve back into me, as a pot into clay, a wave into water, a bracelet into gold.

---

### 2.11 — What does not perish

**Sanskrit:** अहो अहं नमो मह्यं विनाशो यस्य नास्ति मे ।  
ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तं जगन्नाशोऽपि तिष्ठतः ॥ २-११ ॥

**Transliteration:** *aho ahaṃ namo mahyaṃ vināśo yasya nāsti me* · *brahmādistambaparyantaṃ jagannāśo'pi tiṣṭhataḥ*

**Translation:** Wonderful am I! I bow to myself, who know no destruction: even when the whole world perishes, from the creator down to a blade of grass, I remain.

---

### 2.15 — Knower, known, and knowing

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं तथा ज्ञाता त्रितयं नास्ति वास्तवम् ।  
अज्ञानाद् भाति यत्रेदं सोऽहमस्मि निरञ्जनः ॥ २-१५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ tathā jñātā tritayaṃ nāsti vāstavam* · *ajñānād bhāti yatredaṃ so'hamasmi nirañjanaḥ*

**Translation:** Knowledge, the known, and the knower: in truth these three do not exist. That in which they appear through ignorance, I am that, the spotless one.

---

### 2.16 — The root of sorrow

**Sanskrit:** द्वैतमूलमहो दुःखं नान्यत्तस्याऽस्ति भेषजम् ।  
दृश्यमेतन् मृषा सर्वमेकोऽहं चिद्रसोमलः ॥ २-१६ ॥

**Transliteration:** *dvaitamūlamaho duḥkhaṃ nānyattasyā'sti bheṣajam* · *dṛśyametan mṛṣā sarvameko'haṃ cidrasomalaḥ*

**Translation:** Duality is the root of sorrow, and there is no other medicine for it than this: all that is seen is unreal, and I am one, pure, the very taste of awareness.

---

### 2.18 — Neither bound nor free

**Sanskrit:** न मे बन्धोऽस्ति मोक्षो वा भ्रान्तिः शान्ता निराश्रया ।  
अहो मयि स्थितं विश्वं वस्तुतो न मयि स्थितम् ॥ २-१८ ॥

**Transliteration:** *na me bandho'sti mokṣo vā bhrāntiḥ śāntā nirāśrayā* · *aho mayi sthitaṃ viśvaṃ vastuto na mayi sthitam*

**Translation:** For me there is neither bondage nor freedom. The illusion has come to rest, with nothing left to stand on. Wonderful! The world rests in me, and yet in truth it does not rest in me.

---

### 2.20 — Only imagination

**Sanskrit:** शरीरं स्वर्गनरकौ बन्धमोक्षौ भयं तथा ।  
कल्पनामात्रमेवैतत् किं मे कार्यं चिदात्मनः ॥ २-२० ॥

**Transliteration:** *śarīraṃ svarganarakau bandhamokṣau bhayaṃ tathā* · *kalpanāmātramevaitat kiṃ me kāryaṃ cidātmanaḥ*

**Translation:** The body, heaven and hell, bondage and freedom, and fear: all this is only imagination. What have I, who am awareness, to do with it?

---

### 2.22 — My only bondage

**Sanskrit:** नाहं देहो न मे देहो जीवो नाहमहं हि चित् ।  
अयमेव हि मे बन्ध आसीद्या जीविते स्पृहा ॥ २-२२ ॥

**Transliteration:** *nāhaṃ deho na me deho jīvo nāhamahaṃ hi cit* · *ayameva hi me bandha āsīdyā jīvite spṛhā*

**Translation:** I am not the body, nor is the body mine. I am not a separate self; I am awareness. My only bondage was this: my craving for life.

---

### 2.25 — The ocean and its waves

**Sanskrit:** मय्यनन्तमहाम्भोधावाश्चर्यं जीववीचयः ।  
उद्यन्ति घ्नन्ति खेलन्ति प्रविशन्ति स्वभावतः ॥ २-२५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *mayyanantamahāmbhodhāvāścaryaṃ jīvavīcayaḥ* · *udyanti ghnanti khelanti praviśanti svabhāvataḥ*

**Translation:** How wonderful! In me, the boundless ocean, the waves of living beings rise, strike one another, play, and sink back, by their own nature.

---

## Chapter 15 — Live Happily

*Aṣṭāvakra speaks again, returning to the same truth from every side.*

### 15.4 — Live happily

**Sanskrit:** न त्वं देहो न ते देहो भोक्ता कर्ता न वा भवान् ।  
चिद्रूपोऽसि सदा साक्षी निरपेक्षः सुखं चर ॥ १५-४ ॥

**Transliteration:** *na tvaṃ deho na te deho bhoktā kartā na vā bhavān* · *cidrūpo'si sadā sākṣī nirapekṣaḥ sukhaṃ cara*

**Translation:** You are not the body, nor is the body yours. You are neither the doer nor the one who enjoys. You are awareness itself, always the witness, needing nothing. Live happily.

---

### 15.5 — The mind is not yours

**Sanskrit:** रागद्वेषौ मनोधर्मौ न मनस्ते कदाचन ।  
निर्विकल्पोऽसि बोधात्मा निर्विकारः सुखं चर ॥ १५-५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *rāgadveṣau manodharmau na manaste kadācana* · *nirvikalpo'si bodhātmā nirvikāraḥ sukhaṃ cara*

**Translation:** Attraction and aversion belong to the mind, and the mind is never yours. You are free of distinctions, awareness itself, unchanging. Live happily.

---

### 15.6 — All beings in the Self

**Sanskrit:** सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं सर्वभूतानि चात्मनि ।  
विज्ञाय निरहङ्कारो निर्ममस्त्वं सुखी भव ॥ १५-६ ॥

**Transliteration:** *sarvabhūteṣu cātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani* · *vijñāya nirahaṅkāro nirmamastvaṃ sukhī bhava*

**Translation:** Knowing yourself in all beings and all beings in yourself, free of “I” and “mine,” be happy.

---

### 15.7 — Be free of fever

**Sanskrit:** विश्वं स्फुरति यत्रेदं तरङ्गा इव सागरे ।  
तत्त्वमेव न सन्देहश्चिन्मूर्ते विज्वरो भव ॥ १५-७ ॥

**Transliteration:** *viśvaṃ sphurati yatredaṃ taraṅgā iva sāgare* · *tattvameva na sandehaścinmūrte vijvaro bhava*

**Translation:** That in which this universe shimmers like waves on the sea, you are that. Of this there is no doubt. You who are awareness, be free of fever.

---

### 15.11 — Let the waves rise

**Sanskrit:** त्वय्यनन्तमहाम्भोधौ विश्ववीचिः स्वभावतः ।  
उदेतु वास्तमायातु न ते वृद्धिर्न वा क्षतिः ॥ १५-११ ॥

**Transliteration:** *tvayyanantamahāmbhodhau viśvavīciḥ svabhāvataḥ* · *udetu vāstamāyātu na te vṛddhirna vā kṣatiḥ*

**Translation:** In you, the boundless ocean, let the waves of the world rise or set by their own nature. You neither gain nor lose.

---

### 15.14 — Gold and ornaments

**Sanskrit:** यत्त्वं पश्यसि तत्रैकस्त्वमेव प्रतिभाससे ।  
किं पृथक् भासते स्वर्णात् कटकाङ्गदनूपुरम् ॥ १५-१४ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yattvaṃ paśyasi tatraikastvameva pratibhāsase* · *kiṃ pṛthak bhāsate svarṇāt kaṭakāṅgadanūpuram*

**Translation:** Whatever you see, you alone appear in it. Do bracelets, armlets, and anklets appear as anything other than gold?

---

### 15.15 — This and not this

**Sanskrit:** अयं सोऽहमयं नाहं विभागमिति सन्त्यज ।  
सर्वमात्मेति निश्चित्य निःसङ्कल्पः सुखी भव ॥ १५-१५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *ayaṃ so'hamayaṃ nāhaṃ vibhāgamiti santyaja* · *sarvamātmeti niścitya niḥsaṅkalpaḥ sukhī bhava*

**Translation:** Give up the division “I am this” and “I am not that.” Be certain that all is the Self, and, free of intention, be happy.

---

### 15.20 — Give up meditation

**Sanskrit:** त्यजैव ध्यानं सर्वत्र मा किञ्चिद् हृदि धारय ।  
आत्मा त्वं मुक्त एवासि किं विमृश्य करिष्यसि ॥ १५-२० ॥

**Transliteration:** *tyajaiva dhyānaṃ sarvatra mā kiñcid hṛdi dhāraya* · *ātmā tvaṃ mukta evāsi kiṃ vimṛśya kariṣyasi*

**Translation:** Give up meditation entirely. Hold nothing in the heart. You are the Self, already free. What will you do by thinking about it?

---

## Chapter 18 — The Peace of the Wise

*Aṣṭāvakra describes the one who has recognized the Self.*

### 18.1 — Homage to peace

**Sanskrit:** यस्य बोधोदये तावत्स्वप्नवद् भवति भ्रमः ।  
तस्मै सुखैकरूपाय नमः शान्ताय तेजसे ॥ १८-१ ॥

**Transliteration:** *yasya bodhodaye tāvatsvapnavad bhavati bhramaḥ* · *tasmai sukhaikarūpāya namaḥ śāntāya tejase*

**Translation:** Homage to that one, whose nature is happiness alone, peaceful and radiant, at the dawning of whose knowing all illusion becomes like a dream.

---

### 18.5 — Not far away

**Sanskrit:** न दूरं न च सङ्कोचाल्लब्धमेवात्मनः पदम् ।  
निर्विकल्पं निरायासं निर्विकारं निरञ्जनम् ॥ १८-५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *na dūraṃ na ca saṅkocāllabdhamevātmanaḥ padam* · *nirvikalpaṃ nirāyāsaṃ nirvikāraṃ nirañjanam*

**Translation:** The place of the Self is not far away, nor is it reached by narrowing down; it is already attained: beyond distinction, effortless, unchanging, spotless.

---

### 18.6 — Sight unveiled

**Sanskrit:** व्यामोहमात्रविरतौ स्वरूपादानमात्रतः ।  
वीतशोका विराजन्ते निरावरणदृष्टयः ॥ १८-६ ॥

**Transliteration:** *vyāmohamātraviratau svarūpādānamātrataḥ* · *vītaśokā virājante nirāvaraṇadṛṣṭayaḥ*

**Translation:** When delusion simply stops and one's own nature is simply recognized, people shine, free of sorrow, their sight unveiled.

---

### 18.7 — Nothing to practice

**Sanskrit:** समस्तं कल्पनामात्रमात्मा मुक्तः सनातनः ।  
इति विज्ञाय धीरो हि किमभ्यस्यति बालवत् ॥ १८-७ ॥

**Transliteration:** *samastaṃ kalpanāmātramātmā muktaḥ sanātanaḥ* · *iti vijñāya dhīro hi kimabhyasyati bālavat*

**Translation:** Knowing that everything is imagination, and that the Self is free and eternal, what is left for the wise to practice, like a child?

---

### 18.20 — Whatever comes to be done

**Sanskrit:** प्रवृत्तौ वा निवृत्तौ वा नैव धीरस्य दुर्ग्रहः ।  
यदा यत्कर्तुमायाति तत्कृत्वा तिष्ठतः सुखम् ॥ १८-२० ॥

**Transliteration:** *pravṛttau vā nivṛttau vā naiva dhīrasya durgrahaḥ* · *yadā yatkartumāyāti tatkṛtvā tiṣṭhataḥ sukham*

**Translation:** Whether acting or resting, the wise one clings to neither. Whatever comes to be done, they do, and remain at ease.

---

### 18.35 — Not known by striving

**Sanskrit:** शुद्धं बुद्धं प्रियं पूर्णं निष्प्रपञ्चं निरामयम् ।  
आत्मानं तं न जानन्ति तत्राभ्यासपरा जनाः ॥ १८-३५ ॥

**Transliteration:** *śuddhaṃ buddhaṃ priyaṃ pūrṇaṃ niṣprapañcaṃ nirāmayam* · *ātmānaṃ taṃ na jānanti tatrābhyāsaparā janāḥ*

**Translation:** Pure, awake, beloved, complete, beyond all diversity, untroubled: those absorbed in practice do not know this Self.

---

### 18.59 — At ease

**Sanskrit:** सुखमास्ते सुखं शेते सुखमायाति याति च ।  
सुखं वक्ति सुखं भुङ्क्ते व्यवहारेऽपि शान्तधीः ॥ १८-५९ ॥

**Transliteration:** *sukhamāste sukhaṃ śete sukhamāyāti yāti ca* · *sukhaṃ vakti sukhaṃ bhuṅkte vyavahāre'pi śāntadhīḥ*

**Translation:** At ease they sit, at ease they sleep, at ease they come and go; at ease they speak, at ease they eat. Even in the business of life, the peaceful mind is at ease.

---

### 18.60 — The great lake

**Sanskrit:** स्वभावाद्यस्य नैवार्तिर्लोकवद् व्यवहारिणः ।  
महाह्रद इवाक्षोभ्यो गतक्लेशः सुशोभते ॥ १८-६० ॥

**Transliteration:** *svabhāvādyasya naivārtirlokavad vyavahāriṇaḥ* · *mahāhrada ivākṣobhyo gatakleśaḥ suśobhate*

**Translation:** One whose nature is free of distress, though they live and work like everyone else, shines like a great lake, untroubled, free of affliction.
