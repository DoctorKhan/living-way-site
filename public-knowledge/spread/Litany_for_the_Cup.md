# Litany for the Cup

### *Recite when the body runs ahead of the hand*

Plain speech. One line per breath. No performance.

---

Hold the cup.  
Do not drink yet.

The tongue runs ahead of the hand.  
Heat has a number if you stay.

Name what rose —  
anger, fear, wanting, certainty dressed as anger.

It rose in me.  
It is not me.

This felt real.  
I may still be wrong.  
Let us see what repeats.

The cup is only a cup.  
Wash the bowl.

---

**When to use:** before you send the message, sign the order, take the swing, believe the feed, or drink while the water still scalds.

**On-page seed:** Nura, keeper — *The Breach*, Ch 3 (*Hold the cup. Do not drink yet.*).
