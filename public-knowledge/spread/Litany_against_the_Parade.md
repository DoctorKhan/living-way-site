# Litany against the Parade

### *Recite when the crowd sells meaning as a product*

Plain speech. One line per breath. Say it alone if you must; better in a room that feels the same split.

---

Other people are excited,  
as though they were at a parade.

I alone don't care.

Other people have what they need.

I alone possess nothing.

Other people are bright.

I alone am dark.

Other people have a purpose.

I alone don't know.

I drift like a wave on the ocean.  
I blow as aimless as the wind.

I am fed by the Mother —  
not by the parade.

---

**When to use:** after the rally, the healing-pilgrim media, the registry queue, the throne offer, the sync chant — whenever everyone else seems to know why they are cheering.

**On-page:** fragments from Book I; full antiphon Book III teahouse gathering. Cherry Cube runs the opposite shape.

**Short pocket form:** *I alone don't know.*
