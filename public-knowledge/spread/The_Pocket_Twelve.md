# The Pocket Twelve

### *Lines that travel — print one per card, post one per day, say one under load*

Copy freely. No prophet. No movement name.

---

## 1

**Let's test it.**

*When:* someone sells certainty — guru, algorithm, registry, prophecy, your own certainty.

*Full form:* *This felt real. I may still be wrong. Let's test it.*

---

## 2

**It rose in me. It is not me.**

*When:* anger, fear, jealousy, wanting — anything that says *this is who I am*.

*Long form:* *Call it weather. You are the sky.*

---

## 3

**I alone don't know.**

*When:* the room wants a clever answer and none is honest.

*Comes from:* the Parade Liturgy — say it alone or with others who feel the same.

---

## 4

**I carry rice.**

*When:* someone asks you to carry their hero story, their miracle, their war.

*Full form:* *I don't carry heroes on this boat. I carry rice.*

---

## 5

**Information is freight; silence is ballast.**

*When:* you're about to drop a secret for drama, or withhold what the carrier needs.

*Add:* *Told first — every time.*

---

## 6

**The map is not the territory.**

*When:* the file, the model, the prophecy, or the chart feels more real than the ground.

---

## 7

**Meet me in the flow.**

*When:* the address is gone, the phone is dark, the pin is a trap — find the meal pot, the tide, the convoy pass.

---

## 8

**Wash the bowl.**

*When:* the argument is over, the performance is done, the seeking exhausted you.

*Means:* ordinary work. No sermon. Water off the rim.

---

## 9

**No miracles owed.**

*When:* the crowd names you chosen, cursed, or indispensable.

*From Somchai:* *I am the son of a man. I am not a son of anyone who owes you miracles.*

---

## 10

**Forgive the past. Don't rehire it.**

*When:* you're ready to set down an old hurt — and someone expects you to invite the fire back.

---

## 11

**Heat has a number if you stay.**

*When:* the cup, the urge to react, hot tea — hold the hand still before the mouth moves.

---
